# Hexfield 331 — Templates that evolve rather than repeat

A promotional poster, advertisement, or logo is not a frozen image template. Hexfield now treats **what the artwork is for** as a formal constraint, and inherits its layout in the same creative lineage as paint strokes and individually morphing typography.

## Simple studio surface

One new control appears underneath the same live painting canvas: **DESIGN FOR** → Autonomous Art / Promotion or Event / Advertisement / Logo or Identity. There are no new pages, tabs, external accounts or template galleries. The default remains autonomous art. The **existing single words field** optionally accepts `HEADLINE | DETAILS | ACTION` for promotion and advertising. The user supplies *all* the copy; the painter never fabricates sales offers, product claims, prices or calls to action.

## A living layout genome

Every purpose begins with a sensible visual distribution but its genetic record contains independently mutable title position, width, height, negative-space zone, accent strength and graphic mark family (ribbon, brackets, halo, bars or rule). The next child inherits the **accepted** genome and mutates only a bounded part. Rejected physical candidates cannot establish new ancestors. The painter samples real original pigment discontinuities around nearby layout locations, moving the headline toward less busy space rather than blindly placing text at a fixed pixel coordinate.

The final geometry is executed in the *same image* before whole-canvas W / φ / H judgement: an image-derived contrasting frame or accent mark is painted, the independently mutated letter anatomy produces actual pixel masks at the chosen position, and optional detail and action text are composed at smaller proportions. Text geometry continues to push and be pushed by real underlying edges through the existing coupling engine. No CSS text pasted over the output.

For purposeful designs, a bounded legibility term (at most two percentage points either side of raw W/φ/H) can break near-ties between actually painted finalists. Strict golden qualification still wins. There are no extra full-resolution painting passes and no paid image calls.

**KEEP / REJECT** stores small bounded preference weights for a purpose's graphic accent family. Automatic variation learns which physical template material the person favours, without hardcoding one "best" billboard. Manual purpose changes re-typeset the accepted image immediately and rebase further automatic generations from that real canvas. The exact accepted genome is included in saved recipes for reproducible work.

## Tests and constraints

Tests ensure three useful purposes produce **different actual final pixels** from identical uploaded copy and source, deterministic repeats are pixel-identical, layout children inherit a stable root, title/detail/action text is not invented or changed, purposeful readability affects close-call full-canvas rankings but cannot defeat strict golden qualification, and the one-canvas controls and feedback policy are wired into the production engine.

Layouts are algorithmic arrangements, not guaranteed production-ready advertising compliance or marketing copy. Long text is still constrained for legibility; print export remains a finished raster image handed to an independent printer.
